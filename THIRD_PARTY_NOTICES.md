The land outline uses the Natural Earth I projection in a `0 0 1000 520` viewport. It is a modern, generalized geographic reference, not reconstructed medieval borders or coastlines. Approximate city coordinates serve navigation rather than archaeological surveying. The SVG was produced with `d3-geo@3.1.1` and `topojson-client@3.1.0`; those build tools are not embedded in the SVG. See the [original transformation record](prototype/CONTENT_SOURCES.md#geographic-asset).

The Met adapter fetches JSON from the official object endpoint only. It does not fetch images, essays, private APIs or linked resources. An artwork's `isPublicDomain` flag is an image/work status, not the sole basis for permission to reuse the basic collection metadata. Imported metadata remains subject to editorial review and can be incomplete or revised. The Met does not endorse GitWorld.

Raw acquisitions, review packages, operational reports and user databases remain local. A future decision to distribute a downloaded work, image or source excerpt needs a separate rights review of that exact work. Adding a citation or passing schema validation does not provide that permission.
