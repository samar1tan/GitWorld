## Cliopatria observations

**Cliopatria contributors, Seshat Global History Databank**, release **v0.2.1**, commit [`5f433377139a6eeaeacbdc894b70f8805b72c8b5`](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria/tree/5f433377139a6eeaeacbdc894b70f8805b72c8b5). The dataset is offered under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as stated in its [fixed license](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria/blob/5f433377139a6eeaeacbdc894b70f8805b72c8b5/LICENSE.md). The selected official dataset archive has SHA-256 `e10a4e429fca708788ff9e7572a95fce801fae55852d217cffabc1c59cd4eed4`.

GitWorld selects bounded rows, normalizes observation frames into provisional objects and unreviewed claims, derives representative map points, and displays mapped fields and evidence. Its normalization does not establish canonical political identity, continuous sovereignty or ancestry. The upstream authors do not endorse GitWorld.

The comparison's source `Area` values are **542,066.564 km²** for Western Wei and **623,759.799 km²** for Eastern Wei. The displayed source observations apply over **534–535** and **534–545**, respectively. These are the source's geographic observation intervals, not independently established political lifetimes. The screenshot retains the unreviewed status and source-year precision.
