# A usable main structure for the China/Japan case

Proposal v0.6 · 25 October 2026 · V7 source bundle available; content and installed acceptance pending

The [coverage inventory](china-japan-coverage.md) remains the source of omissions. This proposal organizes that material into useful investigations; it does not declare the long ruler lists complete or turn the two modern countries into ancient political atoms.

## One structure at different scales

Use four connected levels throughout the span:

1. **Setting:** a time window, geographic/subject scope and available evidence. Period definitions and traditional/archaeological material remain typed separately. A source heading can help narrow a query without becoming a historical state.
2. **Concurrent structures:** the relevant polities, courts and offices, including uncertain and parallel claims. Show the supported fields and missing evidence. V4 separates the 98 source table groupings from the eleven declared offices; the 95 declared subjects remain a starting classification, not a complete ontology.
3. **Changes and relationships:** explicitly evidenced integrations, divisions, legal changes and contacts with participant roles and dates. Keep different meanings distinct; none automatically becomes a Git parent.
4. **Depth:** officeholders, repeated terms, specific claims, documents and source interpretation. Ruler lists belong here as reusable depth beneath the setting, not as the entire macro view.

These are query and content relationships in the ordinary model, not a separate topic page for each period. The learner can move laterally between contemporary structures, deepen into a claim, compare fixed states and return to the original scope.

## First connected depth increment

The [transformation module](../historical-transformations-prd.md) supplies the minimum new shapes through ordinary collections:

- A dated integration event links the existing Western Jin and Eastern Wu contexts by explicit participant roles, source-stated outcome and independently identified phase evidence. Its campaign start alternatives and conclusion remain separate source facts. Existing surrounding states still provide the larger Three Kingdoms/Jin setting.
- Two legal instruments and an enforcement event support changed fields on the existing imperial-throne object. Cabinet and Diet roles remain separate institutions. Comparing 1940 with 1950 changes four legal fields while the holder stays the same; annual aggregation retains both dated regimes in 1947. The assertions describe constitutional text, not effective power.
- The 263 and 266 event objects extend the political sequence through the same participant and mechanism fields. The 708 issue, coin model and recipient remain separate ordinary objects/relations, with earlier Fuhon-sen production evidence and unknown unsupported neighboring-year states.

These increments do not copy arbitrary values across an event or invent inherited fields. Every claim remains unreviewed and includes a fixed-source/extraction profile. The 60-file v7 bundle retains the v6 historical collection unchanged, including its six archaeological definitions, six court/office/text subjects and source-based navigation metadata; it adds ten bilingual derived-source display names bound to exact source records. These additions retain their separate source plans and rights scopes. Unknown raw-work permissions remain separate from reviewed selected facts and original interpretation; original translations, whitepaper and museum prose retain their original status and are excluded. The installed source-acquisition/review journeys remain pending. Raw pages/PDFs and local preparation scripts are not part of the distributed collection.

## Priorities for completion

Verify the connected investigations, period definitions, institutional observations and source-group classification through ordinary installed navigation and evidence. Reconcile older generic authority bindings and retain the explicit remaining parallel-context and effective-authority gaps. The [scope-navigation requirements](../scope-workspace-prd.md) provide user-created contexts and whole-batch organization so the starting collection does not become a fixed set of topic pages; production and installed acceptance remain separate from that design.

At every step, assess whether a learner can identify the setting, compare meaningful fields, follow a relationship and explain the evidence limit using the shipped application. Counts, source access, mapping tests and a prepared candidate do not pass C02–C06. New evidence acquisition and rule inspection must enter the ordinary preview → review/adopt → Changes → stage → commit flow.
