# China and Japan: the v0.1 acceptance case

Revision **v0.1** · 25 October 2026 · All case results pending installed acceptance

The idea of following China and Japan across their histories is a product starting point for GitWorld. This is the principal end-to-end acceptance case for v0.1, from evidenced ancient coverage to the present. Tang-period connections are one deep investigation within it. They do not stand in for either history or for the complete software workflow.

This case implements the [main PRD](../PRD.md), [data-workspace requirements](../data-workspace-prd.md), [learning workflow](../learning-workflow.md), [onboarding](../onboarding-prd.md) and [settings](../settings-prd.md). A long reign list, an attractive map or a successful mapping script cannot pass the case on its own.

## Historical coverage contract

Maintain an explicit versioned inventory of periods, objects, concurrent governing structures, continuity/split/integration relations, institutional changes, cross-regional contacts, sources, mapped assertions, usable states and evidence gaps. Identify the date through which present-day material was checked. No silent gap at the end of a source table counts as present-day coverage.

China's inventory must account for ancient evidence and disputed/traditional coverage; major successive and concurrent political structures; periods of division, integration and overlapping rule; imperial and republican structures; and modern/contemporary governments and institutions. Audit major parallel regimes as well as the familiar succession headings. Dates and labels from a geographic snapshot or catalog period are not automatically a polity's lifetime.

Japan's inventory must account for archaeological/period definitions without turning them into invented states; ancient court formation; successive and concurrent court, warrior and other governing institutions; divided or overlapping authority; institutional changes into the modern period; and contemporary coverage. A complete imperial genealogy alone is insufficient to describe government or historical change. Separate repeated tenures and parallel courts rather than collapsing people into one uninterrupted interval.

For both histories, the inventory must name every important omitted period or parallel structure, its effect on queries and the work needed to fill it. The release requires a coherent usable main structure across the full span. The accepted abstraction principle permits documented compression; it does not permit unexplained multi-century holes, a handful of preset nodes or presenting all history as one timeless national entity.

Historical objects and views retain multiple levels: broad spatial/temporal setting; periods/processes; institutions and political structures; dated observations and assertions; supporting evidence. Users choose and change scope without learning internal IDs or opening a bespoke topic page. China and Japan use the same rendering, mapping and query machinery as any other imported subject.

The [coverage inventory](china-japan-coverage.md) records the current fixed sources, mapped structure, query consequences, new candidates and unpassed content requirements. A candidate is not an update to the installed bundle until reviewed and adopted.

## Depth and content review

Review a complete investigation at each relevant scale, not one row per dynasty. Verify that the selected state has useful supported fields and an understandable next investigation; names/dates alone do not establish that depth. Curated explanations and relations require evidence and modeling notes, while repeated structured material is mapped deterministically rather than written as a fixed demonstration.

Rowan owns coverage/source interpretation; root owns the integrated case. Casey independently inspects discovery, section roles, copy and installed navigation; Morgan reviews state/revision/recovery semantics; Quinn reviews publication rights and the exact source/installer assets. An agent's successful run is interface/engineering evidence. Human learning validation must be reported separately and truthfully.

## Release decision

All C01–C12 are required alongside D, M, J and S requirements. Content review, unit/integration checks, installed journeys and human studies remain distinct evidence. An unpassed required step blocks completion of the delivery goal. Preserve exact limitations and fixes; do not mark the case complete from a bulk row count or a generated report.
