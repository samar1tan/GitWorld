# Source identity, role and chronology bindings

Document v0.1 · 25 October 2026 · Module requirements; implementation and installed acceptance are separate

## User need

A learner importing a ruler table should be able to explore the relevant polity, court or office, compare its supported states and inspect how the source was interpreted. A table organized by a historical period does not make that period a state or country. Shared names and external identifiers do not establish identity. Repeated terms and concurrent courts must survive the conversion.

This module extends the [data workspace](data-workspace-prd.md) and the [China/Japan acceptance case](acceptance/china-japan-v0.1.md). It supplies reusable deterministic binding rules and reviewable sidecar data. It does not add an AI service or require a person to construct a page for every imported object.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs are an acquired knowledge collection and an explicit versioned binding plan. The plan identifies exact source objects/properties, source revisions, optional assertion selections, target objects and types, transformation direction, evidence and rights. Source-local identities, original statements, raw snapshots, evidence and review state remain intact.

Outputs are the original collection plus typed objects and derived assertions, a reproducible binding source record, rule-level results and an exception queue. Every derived assertion identifies its original claim, binding rule/version and both source and interpretation evidence. New interpretations start unreviewed. Re-running the same plan creates no duplicate claims; a changed plan or upstream source cannot overwrite an earlier interpretation silently.

Supported types distinguish polities, courts, offices, source-defined periods, traditional/contested subjects and chronology candidates. Stable identifiers and types are independent of either UI language. A declared source identity can support a match; a string comparison of names cannot perform a merge. One modern country must not become the permanent container for all ancient material.

## Binding behavior

Rules select exact source identities and revisions. They can project a subject/property to a declared historical subject/property or reverse an explicitly identified membership/held-office claim. Assertion selections support reviewed role exceptions, such as concurrent courts in one published table. No rule infers political ancestry from table order, a succession field, biological descent or a matching name. Historical splits, integration and influence require their own typed evidence.

An explicit identity decision may also map a source-local entity value to a declared target identity, with its own evidence and target type. For example, a table's title cell may lack a link while its separate personal-name cell identifies the person. The derived assertion records both identities and the decision; the original provisional object/value remains unchanged. An unused or invalid identity decision must be reported, rather than silently matching another name.

Role bindings must not assign a person's entire tenure to a role that applied only during part of it. A source's combined interval remains combined until there is separate dated evidence for a subdivision. Different institutions and parallel claims remain distinct. A source's first/last available row is a coverage boundary, not a foundation or collapse.

Missing source objects, changed revisions, unknown assertion selections, incompatible target types, rejected source claims and conflicting rules remain actionable exceptions. A binding must not discard an original claim merely because it cannot be projected. No row is adopted as confirmed history by running the mapper.

## Competing chronology

A table may publish alternative reign chronologies or only a speculative reign length. Each selected source chronology remains identifiable, with its original column label, date text, source location and any parseable interval. A length such as “45” cannot become the year 45. Approximate or incomplete text does not receive an invented midpoint, margin or calendar conversion.

Parseable alternative intervals can produce conservative **possible** state membership, keeping each chronology separate. These projections do not establish a definite tenure or event. The original candidate interval remains available independently of the possible-state envelope. One-year candidates also remain possible. Conflicting candidates reach the existing `possibleValues`/`uncertain` state path; the mapper never chooses a preferred chronology by source rank or column order. Unsupported dates remain searchable chronology material with their source text and explicit unknown time.

Use `validTime.certainty: "possible"` with the complete published start/end for that projection. This describes temporal applicability, not the claim's overall truth or its review status. Default temporal behavior remains unchanged; invalid certainty values fail validation. Do not encode a tenure as an uncertain point or an artificial open boundary merely to obtain an uncertain state. Readers that cannot implement the applicability field must reject it. See the [model contract](data-model.md#temporal-applicability).

The resulting history view may show supported observations and uncertainty. It must not manufacture precise accessions or continuity edges from competing ancient dates. Source order can remain an inspectable ordering of table entries, never a historical parent relation.

## Activity contexts from recorded roles

A learner should be able to follow a source-listed activity from its named people into the relevant recorded polity, court or office, and find the same activity from that context. A list of envoys with isolated personal names is insufficient. The smallest increment is a deterministic join of an activity's explicitly listed person and an independently recorded, dated role held by that same stable person identity.

The input plan selects exact source identities/revisions, listed-person properties, holder properties and allowed context types/identity providers. It also declares the forward and inverse property names, modeling evidence, temporal policy, rights and allowed input review states. Names, a person's lifespan, a source table's coverage, a matching country name or record order cannot satisfy the join. A raw source-defined tenure scope is not automatically a political institution; the plan must identify which typed declared contexts are eligible.

For each compatible pair, retain both original assertion IDs, both source snapshots/revisions/hashes, the original temporal scopes and the computed overlap. This first projection is explicitly at civil-year resolution. It does not claim a more precise meeting date. Approximate dates, a single open boundary, sub-year precision that cannot establish a definite overlap, different source calendars and possible applicability remain possible or unresolved. Open tenures are never extended indefinitely. Source date values without applicability do not supply a role interval.

The output is a separate modeling source and unreviewed forward/inverse assertions. No input assertion, source or review status changes. Multiple supported contexts remain multiple; missing identity or time appears in a bounded, countable exception queue rather than being guessed. A cancelled or outcome-unknown activity retains that source status. A listed sender is not automatically a participant, a completed journey, a direct meeting or a transmitter of an institution. The projection creates no continuity, split, integration or influence edge.

An unchanged plan is reproducible and adds no duplicates. A changed source revision, incompatible identity/type or conflicting prior projection is reported without overwriting it. Projection fields and explanatory labels have English and Simplified Chinese presentation. New candidates and any field vocabulary remain separate from the frozen installed bundle until independent content/rights review and ordinary adoption. Developer validation does not pass the installed case.

Acceptance requires an unprepared synthetic source and the frozen source collection to use the same code; exact two-sided evidence and year overlap; retained cancellation, unknown time, unresolved identity and multiple contexts; no age-based or name-based join; repeated application without duplicates; and old/source records unchanged. This increment supports C06 context navigation. C04/C05 still require separately evidenced political and institutional relationships, while C06 still requires documented transmission and recipient changes beyond these listed contacts.

## Authority-qualified table endpoints

A table can arrange de jure and de facto endpoints on different physical rows under one row-spanning holder cell. Pairing each physical row as one tenure can combine incompatible bases and hide a supported first holder. The source mapper must group by the exact holder-cell anchor and match start/end cells by an explicit authority basis, rather than by the person's name or row order. Repeated terms with different holder-cell anchors remain separate.

The profile pins the source snapshot, table index and complete headers, holder/start/end columns, an existing typed context and the separate output field for each basis. An explicit cell label takes precedence over its column heading. An unqualified cell may inherit the one explicit basis in its row, or the explicit bases of the rows actually covered by that cell's rowspan, only when its column supplies no basis of its own. Missing, competing or incompatible endpoints remain exceptions. A birth/death pair is never a tenure.

Keep de jure and de facto results in different fields. A source authority survey is not a formal shogunal institution: its context type and field labels must preserve that distinction. The mapper must not assign a military office to a person merely because a source places that person in a comparative table. Preserve each endpoint's original text, cell locator, authority label and date precision, the source revision/hash, and the assignment rule. New facts and interpretations remain unreviewed.

Create a separately hashed facts source containing only the selected holder identity and endpoint cells, provenance, compatible rights and transformation notes. Exclude article HTML, portraits and prose. New source candidates and bilingual field vocabulary require independent review before replacing or extending the installed collection. Existing unqualified bindings remain visible for explicit reconciliation; an additive qualified-field candidate must not silently pretend it has rebuilt all earlier mappings.

Acceptance includes a synthetic crossed-row table, shared endpoints, missing/ambiguous bases, repeated terms, an authority survey that remains a survey, exact source/header/hash mismatch rejection, and the fixed cached source. The Tokugawa test must resolve the source's qualified 1603–1605 formal interval and 1600–1616 de facto interval separately, without borrowing life dates. This is source-mapping evidence, not a claim that all political authority or C05 is complete.

## Installed mapping boundary

The activity-context and qualified-endpoint implementations currently produce ordinary collection candidates through pure ingestion functions. Their local preparation and verification scripts are curator tools. They do not provide an installed source-acquisition or rule-review journey, and their output must not be described as that journey's acceptance.

The next service integration must normalize acquired material, apply the explicitly selected source-identity bindings, then project qualified endpoints and activity contexts before the existing collection preview/adoption boundary. A context join may read a fixed library revision together with the new source selection; its preview must identify that revision and must not silently persist unrelated library objects. A partial result uses append-observations so omitted claims survive. Preview and adoption must use the same source hashes, rule versions and input fingerprint. Source acquisition currently supports different provider-specific paths; an existing Wikidata-only collector must not be presented as a working Wikipedia collector.

Ship mapping profiles as inspectable versioned data, separate from renderer routing. The review view must identify the provider and pinned source revision/hash, selected tables/headers/columns, declared context types and identities, field semantics, date/basis policy, evidence and exception counts. A built-in profile can choose these explicit rules for a known source; it cannot silently accept a changed header or source revision, merge people by name, upgrade historical review, or imply that matching table boundaries establish an institution. Users must be able to inspect the resulting claims and retain or defer exceptions before the ordinary Changes → stage → commit path. The same generic projection code must accept another compatible reviewed profile without a new case page.

## Review and local operation

The existing reconciliation inspector shows the proposed type/identity, source claim, binding evidence and resulting fields. A learner can retain source material, apply a repeated rule to the previewed selection or defer an exception, then use the ordinary Changes → stage → commit path. Batch adoption is a local research action, distinct from historical confirmation and public redistribution approval. A rule change has a before/after preview and retains the old repository revision.

Both UI locales must identify these states and actions. Historical names, original citations and translations remain content with provenance, not UI identifiers. An unavailable content translation uses the existing English fallback; source-language names do not count as finished bilingual preset copy.

Bundled presets must complete their English and Simplified Chinese display labels before release. Reusable source-label acquisition may follow an exact English or Japanese article identity through its published Chinese language link, then use the Chinese wiki's explicit `zh-cn` variant title. These are explicit supported providers, not arbitrary renderer-supplied hosts. Preserve both page identities/revisions, redirects, retrieval time, source hashes and attribution. Traditional Chinese, Japanese text and an untranslated English fallback are not counted as Simplified Chinese completion. Names without a supported counterpart enter a review queue.

Label lookup uses bounded metadata-only batches, fixed provider hosts, cancellation and the same backpressure rules as other acquisition. It neither downloads article prose/images nor changes historical time. Editorial translations of period/role labels and composed chronology labels are identified as translations, with the original source wording retained independently. Label records and their evidence remain separate from historical state assertions. A missing translation in a bundled preset is a release gap, even when English fallback is appropriate for subsequently imported research material.

Source text values that appear in the preset's normal state/relation views, such as a cabinet label or a listed group of envoys, also need independently stored display translations. Bind these to the source assertion and a hash of its actual value. Do not replace the canonical source value, change a state comparison or use translated prose as an identifier. A shared formatter may follow the recorded derivation chain when the value is unchanged. Conflicting or stale display translations remain explicit rather than silently choosing one.

Normal installed operation must offer the mapping/review path through shipped controls and commands. Developer scripts used to prepare or verify a preset do not satisfy this requirement. Cached source evidence and applied bindings remain available offline.

## Bounded Wikipedia acquisition and mapping increment

The next backend increment exposes an English Wikipedia workflow separately from the existing Wikidata collector. A learner selects a page title and may supply a revision number; no renderer-provided URL, host, HTML or executable mapping is accepted. A successful current-page acquisition immediately pins the returned page identity, revision and canonical snapshot hash. A requested revision must belong to the selected page. Redirect/title normalization must be explicit, and a mismatched page, missing revision, changed archived bytes or invalid checkpoint must stop rather than silently substitute current content.

Acquire one page per durable source job. Preserve its request, resolved identity, snapshot, source permissions, acquisition receipt and failures in the local archive. Request identity and accepted snapshots survive restart. A completed job reuses its stored snapshot; retrying an incomplete job does not replace already accepted material. Cancellation retains recoverable work. Respect maxlag, rate limits, response budgets and cancellation without an automatic retry loop. Local source acquisition is not historical adoption or publication approval.

Inspection returns bounded table metadata, complete selected headers, row counts and a small original-language cell sample, never active HTML. Offer two versioned mapping families: explicitly selected holder/tenure columns, and explicitly selected dated-entry/attribute columns. Their column choices and source-defined scope are inspectable data. They do not identify canonical institutions by page name, equate lifespan with tenure, translate arbitrary source content or infer political ancestry, completion or causation. Unsupported dates remain unknown with exceptions. A second compatible table/profile must work without a new source-title condition or renderer page.

An explicit holder-column selection controls both the recorded name and any linked identity. The mapper must not borrow a temple, posthumous, dynastic or other name/link from an unselected column. A readable selected name without a usable link remains a provisional identity with an exception; an empty selected holder produces an unresolved-row exception rather than adopting another column's person. A provisional identity belongs to the exact source snapshot and selected cell anchor, not to a person inferred from the same row position in another column or revision. The older automatic-column mapper is a separate compatibility path, not proof that explicit selection was honored.

Validate named Gregorian and Julian civil dates against their own month lengths and leap-year rules before assigning applicability, including open-start tenures. Compare known month/day components when both endpoints share a year. Do not switch calendars at a regional reform date, convert Julian days to Gregorian days, infer a missing calendar or invent a year zero. Leap calculations for BCE use the corresponding astronomical year only internally; the source's historical year, calendar label and raw text remain intact. For an unspecified calendar, dates valid under both supported civil rules may retain their unspecified label, while a calendar-dependent leap date remains uncertain. An unsupported named calendar cannot receive applicability from this civil-date parser. Invalid or unresolvable dates retain raw text and an exception without a validTime. These are calendar-consistency rules, not confirmation that a source's date is historically correct. See [USNO leap-year rules](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/leap_years) and [year numbering](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/millennium).

English source dates support both `D Month YYYY` and `Month D, YYYY` (also the month-first form without its comma), with the same optional CE/BCE suffix and the same civil-date validation. Whitespace and line breaks may separate the date parts; the original date text remains in evidence. A user selecting a source's start/end columns must not need a different mapping profile merely because one English article uses month-first dates. This does not add locale-dependent guessing, ordinal-day parsing, another calendar conversion or a page-specific rule.

Explicit numeric `Y-MM-DD` dates also support one to four year digits, including unpadded three-digit historical years, with two-digit month and day and an optional CE/BCE suffix. Their internal hyphens are date syntax, not interval separators. They use the same civil-date validation and retain their original year, calendar, day precision and raw evidence; year zero and invalid month/day combinations do not receive applicability. Numeric dates may also be interval endpoints. Approximate source markers such as `c. 1920 BC` remain uncertain. This parser does not use a platform date parser, timezone conversion or automatic date correction.

A mapping preview pins the acquired snapshot hash, exact headers/columns, profile/version, calendar policy, complete collection, field/type/value vocabulary, exceptions, original source permissions and the actual library HEAD. Source-derived labels retain their language; the product-authored profile and vocabulary labels have English and Simplified Chinese presentation. The preview is persisted through the existing version-2 paired importer. Adoption reuses those bytes and its prepared identifier; it must not fetch or remap a moving source. Preserve unknown permissions. Raw page HTML remains in the acquisition archive; the adopted source ledger keeps selected facts, their original locators, exact upstream identity/revision/hash and transformation notes.

Permission evidence also binds the metadata response hash, original site-rights observation/hash, retrieval time and request URL. Its presence records what the source reported; it does not upgrade an unrecognized or unknown permission. The original archived snapshot remains unchanged when that observation is attached to the mapping plan.

Use the existing append-observations, working-content/HEAD guards, prepared/completed receipt and pending/resume path. There is one repository HEAD. A partially written adoption survives restart without the previous page token, fetch connection or renderer memory. Complete library backups include the optional source workspace and validate its immutable snapshots before replacement. The old collection-only, Wikidata and public bulk-source contracts remain supported.

After a normalization-rule correction, a not-yet-accepted preview from the superseded profile version requires a new review using the cached source. Do not silently remap it during adoption or accept known obsolete rules. Already accepted prepared/completed receipts retain their original bytes and recover through the existing contract; rule updates do not rewrite that accepted operation.

Backend acceptance includes two synthetic profile configurations, a real fixed China/Japan page, exact-page/revision checks, changed source/header rejection, unknown-rights preservation, cancellation/backpressure/retry, immutable previews, ordinary Git state/query consumption, interrupted adoption and no-fetch restart recovery. This increment does not itself pass C08: shipped profile selection, source/exception review, adoption, recovery, bilingual interaction and native installation still require their own integrated evidence.

The backend increment defines `wikipedia.collect`, `jobs`, `inspect`, `preview`, `issues` and `adopt` in the [typed contract](../packages/contracts/src/wikipedia-types.ts). Collection returns a durable job even after source failure, exposing its receipt for recovery. Inspection and mapping read the archived snapshot offline. Preview exposes the exact plan, a bounded proposed-claim/source-header sample, the complete bounded vocabulary and the exception total; source rows, mapped rows, objects, claims, date-bearing claims and applicability-bearing claims have separate counts. The shared paired preview also reports its projection exceptions. A profile does not run another source request at adoption.

Budgets are one page, 20 seconds per request, 512 KiB identity/rights metadata, 8 MiB parse response, ten selected tables, 10,000 total source rows and 64 columns. Mapping also respects the existing 32 MiB paired artifact, 16 MiB repository record and 40,000-path write budgets before adoption. Preview samples contain at most thirty claims and forty relevant objects. Its first thirty exception details are an initial sample; the complete bounded report must be reachable through `wikipedia.issues`, with pages of at most one hundred. The read request binds the job, prepared preview and input fingerprint. Each response includes the same report hash, total and page bounds; it reads the stored report offline without refetching or remapping, including after a normalizer update or later repository commit. A changed or corrupt binding is an error, never a replacement report. The interface retains the current issue page and its selections when a page read fails, offers an explicit retry, and discards late results for another preview. Tests must reach entries after thirty, restart offline, reject exchanged fingerprints/jobs and verify that report reading does not change repository content or adoption state. This read capability remains pending implementation and integrated acceptance until its evidence is recorded.

Original source text is not automatically translated. A source permission observation outside the recognized license URL remains unknown, including an unrecognized localized license-deed URL; local adoption does not convert it into publication approval. MediaWiki [query normalization](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Query) and [revision parsing](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Parsing_wikitext) provide the transport identity; an old revision ID alone does not prove that it belongs to the title entered by the user.

## Distribution and local reading

A bundled research collection may contain several ordinary `KnowledgeCollection` files. Partition by source provenance and complete subject bundles, keeping a shared object/source catalog where necessary. Every distributed file must remain within the existing source publication gate; do not weaken that gate or hide the collection in an archive. A pinned bundle manifest records each file's byte hash, the merged collection fingerprint, source content hashes, counts, field vocabulary and attribution sidecar. Normal identity-based collection merging must reproduce the same objects and assertions without conflict. Shared references are references, not a reason to infer a historical relation.

The trusted installer reads only pinned bundle paths and hashes. An independently selected user file uses a separate, bounded standard-collection parser, then the same preview, review choice, reconciliation, working changes and commit guards as source acquisition. Neither a bundle nor a shape-valid file confirms historical assertions. Knowledge collections are distinct from authored history-model packages; selecting one must not silently route it through the other package editor.

An adoption response may include at most forty navigation labels for its present changed/unchanged objects, alongside their complete identifier lists and the total unique navigation count. This is a result sample, not a full distribution summary. Unchanged objects retaining a local name correction use that local label; a preserved local deletion must not produce a link to an absent object. Conflicts remain separate from successful navigation results.

The local service supplies display metadata at the same repository revision as the historical records. Return source headers, the relevant entity/field/type labels, and assertion-value display text with actual language, missing/conflict status and translation evidence. Do not send whole source records or the complete label ledger to a renderer for one selected object. Keep canonical values, identifiers and temporal/review semantics intact. Field vocabulary is generic presentation metadata, not a region-specific routing rule. Unrecognized imported fields retain their identifiers rather than acquiring fabricated labels.

The initial object overview summarizes the complete set matching the current query, before pagination. Show bounded type counts and dated applicability coverage as direct scope choices, with missing time/location counts. Distinguish dates carried as source values from claims with historical applicability; neither is a count of historical events, a continuous history or a confidence estimate. Temporal buckets count distinct objects intersecting each bucket, so one object can occur in several buckets. Keep arbitrary source types and unknown material accessible. The map remains an explicit view of available coordinates and labels a page-limited set accurately; a collection without coordinates must not open as a blank world map. No map point or political ancestry may be invented to fill this overview.

English and Simplified Chinese render the same state and evidence. A missing translation retains the original language; conflicting translations retain the original value and expose the conflict. Translation evidence identifies the display transformation separately from historical evidence. The service owns hashing and derivation traversal; browser modules must not import Node cryptography. A working-tree display cache must observe actual working content changes, including a label-only correction at an unchanged HEAD. Locale changes only redraw the selected revision.

Acceptance for this bridge requires: all bundle files pass the unchanged size bound; ordinary merging reproduces the frozen semantic fingerprint; changed bytes, unsafe paths and conflicting shard identities fail; plain same-schema imports use preview/adoption guards; translated, missing, conflicting and stale value text behaves correctly; source headers exclude raw records; and a working-label correction is visible without a commit. Render and operate the selected-object/state/evidence path in both locales. These checks do not pass the full historical-coverage or installed C01–C12 journeys.

## Acceptance

1. Map an acquired source scope to a declared polity/court/office without changing or deleting the original records. Inspect the rule version and claim-level source/interpretation evidence.
2. Reject a changed source revision or unknown assertion selection; preserve recoverable source material and return the exact rule exception.
3. Preserve two terms held by the same person and two concurrent court roles; do not merge them by name or invent a parent edge.
4. Compare two supported states through the ordinary state/diff path. Retain multiple, unknown and unreviewed values.
5. Import two parseable competing chronologies. Inspect their separate intervals and possible state values; no column becomes the automatic true chronology. A speculative length and an unsupported approximate date remain undated material.
6. Re-run an unchanged plan without duplicate objects/assertions. A conflicting interpretation remains visible rather than overwriting a source or prior rule.
7. Walk the binding preview, exception handling, commit, reopen and evidence inspection in both installed UI locales. Record every external intervention. Pure-module tests or a prepared preset alone do not pass this journey.

The implementation handoff must report the tested source revisions, actual object/assertion/chronology counts, unresolved role/type bindings, translation gaps and installed-journey status. Coverage targets remain requirements until those checks occur.
