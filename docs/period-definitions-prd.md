# Source-specific period definitions

Document v0.2 · 25 October 2026 · Bounded content proposal; no installed acceptance

## User outcome and scope

A learner choosing an ancient setting needs archaeological context before opening individual rulers. A period definition supplies a source's label, approximate temporal extent and geographic lookup scope. It does not establish a polity, a civilization's origin, a population's identity or a continuous political state.

This increment supports the ancient-setting gap in the [China/Japan coverage inventory](acceptance/china-japan-coverage.md). Evaluate a small set of definitions before extending the corpus: Yangshao, Longshan and Liangzhu culture definitions, and Jōmon, Yayoi and Kofun period definitions. Keep competing definitions separate. Do not add a ruler list, coordinates, ancestry, a new navigation mode or a source provider in this content increment.

## Inputs and source evaluation

Reuse the existing PeriodO adapter with a pinned dataset revision, byte hash, retrieval time, authority identifier and period identifier. A bibliographic authority is the definition's source, not a certification of historical correctness. Read the cited source where available and record any difference from its PeriodO transcription.

The inspected dataset includes the British Museum material-culture vocabulary and the Nara National Research Institute for Cultural Properties' 2022 *Style Manual for English Texts: General Conventions*, appendix page 61. The manual's other institutional tables illustrate why a single consensus boundary must not be synthesized. The [PeriodO dedication](https://perio.do/license/) covers contributors' rights; it does not establish permission to redistribute all underlying publications or their expression. The [Nara source record](https://sitereports.nabunken.go.jp/en/115465) makes the manual accessible, while the institute's [site policy](https://www.nabunken.go.jp/info/law.html) does not supply a general open license for that work. The British Museum's dedicated data-service terms require separate verification from its other website terms. These proposed authorities remain uncleared for an installed preset.

ROAD has an independently published CC BY-SA 4.0 data policy, described by its authors in [section 5.3](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0289513). Its deep-Palaeolithic definitions are a different scope. They cannot substitute for missing Neolithic cultures or the named Japanese periods merely because their license is clearer.

The bounded alternative is Wikidata's structured item data, covered by its [CC0 policy](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing). Inspect only six identified concepts: [Jōmon](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q52824), [Yayoi](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q207858), [Kofun](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q459939), [Yangshao](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q854631), [Longshan](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1057935) and [Liangzhu](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1204909). This does not license linked images, upstream articles or talk-page prose. Public item-page inspection found separate start/end statements for the Japanese periods and a circa qualifier for Jōmon. The Chinese item pages did not show equivalent date statements; an English description containing years cannot substitute for structured boundary evidence. A successful fixed-entity snapshot remains required before confirming those fields or deriving content. The bounded background API attempt returned maxlag with a failed receipt and zero snapshots; it produced no candidate.

## Model and mapping contract

- Use stable `periodo:` identities and type `periodo:period-definition`. A name match never merges two authorities' definitions or binds one to an existing polity.
- Preserve source language, original label, authority, bibliographic locator, source revision, raw-record hash and mapping version. English and Simplified Chinese authored labels and explanatory vocabulary must have explicit translation provenance. They do not replace original source labels.
- Retain original start/stop labels and numeric boundary ranges independently. Normalize XSD astronomical years to civil BCE numbering through the existing adapter; do not infer a historical calendar conversion or use a source publication date as an ancient date.
- A century range stays a range. “Circa” remains approximate; a numeric year in a source does not remove that qualification. Any later distributable projection must represent uncertain temporal applicability as possible, or leave it unknown when no supported interpretation is available. It must record that rule separately from unmodified normalization.
- A definition's temporal scope means “within this published definition,” not that all described material, institutions or people existed throughout the interval. Missing, open or incompatible boundaries remain explicit. Other definitions remain accessible rather than being replaced with an averaged boundary.
- Spatial identifiers are source lookup references. Modern China/Japan or island identifiers do not establish ancient jurisdiction, territorial extent or membership. Do not manufacture map points from them. Unresolved target references remain visible gaps.
- Ordinary state, comparison and evidence paths may consume the same typed assertions once adopted. Comparing definitions reveals different source classifications or dates; it must not present an archaeological label change as a political transfer.

## Bounded Wikidata projection contract

The existing statement mapper preserves Wikidata entity types, statement identifiers, values, rank, references and raw qualifiers. It does not turn `P580`/`P582` main values into a definition interval, and its property-name mapping does not establish a new object type. Keep that normal output unchanged. Derive a separate `gitworld:period-definition` object through an explicit versioned plan rather than relabeling the culture or period concept itself.

The plan must pin the source item identity, fixed revision, snapshot hash and exact original assertion identifiers for type, start, end and any selected spatial reference. Its output records the original-to-selected-facts-to-plan-to-derived-assertion chain. The derived definition links to the original concept; neither a modern country reference nor an archaeological culture becomes a political institution. Use stable identities for the selected source boundary set; changing display language does not change them.

Pair boundaries only when the plan can justify that they belong to the same definition, such as an explicitly selected singular start/end set in the fixed source. Multiple competing boundaries remain separate supported alternatives or exceptions; do not build a Cartesian product, choose a preferred rank as historical truth, or average dates. Preserve each boundary's statement GUID, precision, calendar, uncertainty, reference and source revision. A circa qualifier makes the derived interval possible; it never disappears because the numeric boundary is a single year. An unsupported before/after uncertainty, missing boundary or ambiguous pairing leaves applicability unknown and creates an inspectable exception. A point-in-time qualifier on a focus-list statement is not an archaeological boundary. `P571`/`P576` cannot be substituted for `P580`/`P582` without an explicit source-supported semantic rule.

Generic properties need EN/zh-CN presentation for the defined concept, source definition label, start, end and spatial lookup reference, plus projection-rule and derived-from evidence. All derived claims remain unreviewed. An entity's source dates remain time-valued facts; only the separately declared definition interval can support definition-label/scope applicability. No missing Chinese date is filled from another culture, a description or an adjacent period. Missing and noncontinuous coverage are useful visible results.

This is a projection requirement, not a claim that a new production projector exists. Reuse the existing generic normalized-assertion, binding and evidenced-field mechanisms where they satisfy these guards. An unavailable upstream returns a durable failed/partial acquisition receipt and pending identities. Keep retry/backoff and cancellation behavior; do not fabricate a snapshot, remove maxlag for background work, or add an alternate unbounded provider to make the content appear complete.

## Fixed Wikipedia selected-fact increment

Following the failed Wikidata acquisition, inspect the same six concepts through a separately identified source: fixed editions of the English Wikipedia articles. This is a source change with its own evidence and CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution, not a retry or bypass of Wikidata. The existing fixed-host page acquisition and generic `inspectPublishedFields` / `projectEvidencedFields` chain are sufficient for a local selected-fact candidate. This content operation does not add an installed profile or an article-specific renderer.

Use the infobox `Dates` field where it exists. Where it does not, pin the unique date-bearing paragraph by selector and full normalized text hash. A paragraph is evidence for selected numeric facts; retain neither its prose nor its anchor labels. Keep original-page identity, fixed revision, source hash, actual history/permalink, field locator and selected-text hash. Dates and original bilingual presentation remain attributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Linked images and raw article HTML stay outside the candidate.

The output has at most six source-specific `gitworld:period-definition` objects. It does not replace a culture concept, bind a country, add coordinates or imply that one period inherited another. The minimal fields are `period:definition-start`, `period:definition-end`, `period:definition-label` and `period:chronology-basis`. Boundary time values remain separately inspectable; only a justified paired extent applies the label/basis to a historical year. Original date fragments retain the source calendar as unspecified; civil ordinal-century interpretation is a declared range rule, not a historical calendar conversion.

The preparation plan records deterministic date captures and the range-normalization policy. Approximate ranges have possible applicability. A third-century boundary remains the full civil ordinal-century range 201–300; a word such as “mid” may remain a qualification rather than being narrowed to invented years. Retain multiple explicit chronology alternatives on the same definition with their own rule/basis qualifiers. In the inspected Yayoi article, the conventional year range and the earlier century-based chronology must coexist; do not silently replace either or turn them into a unique consensus. A parser or pairing failure blocks that extent and reports an exception rather than borrowing a year.

Evidence must demonstrate replay from each pinned source, no retained source prose, complete EN/zh-CN labels/interpretation, compatible ordinary collection and paired-artifact parsing, possible/unknown state behavior, separate alternative chronology and unchanged v5 data. This bounded candidate remains subject to independent content/rights and root review before any bundle inclusion. Ordinary acquisition/profile use and C02 acceptance remain separate.

## Rights and distribution boundary

Normal acquisition and mapping keep an unverified authority's rights unknown. A PeriodO CC0 declaration, official publisher, successful fetch or absence of long prose cannot promote those rights to allowed. The adapter's full authority/period record can contain notes and source expression; keep such output local until its exact retained content is cleared.

A possible later facts-only distribution needs a separate, hash-bound selection and rights decision for the exact short labels, numeric boundaries, references and original bilingual interpretation. Exclude raw pages/PDFs, images, source notes and creative prose unless separately licensed. Preserve the unknown normalization and source-work permissions. That review must not claim the underlying work was licensed or the historical content was confirmed. If no suitable distributable scope is established, record the source/coverage gap and stop this content increment rather than filling it with authored dates.

## Dependencies and acceptance evidence

The adapter is available as a pure mapping module. This proposal does not add an installed PeriodO acquisition/profile control. File loading, a bundled collection and a private preparation script do not pass ordinary source-acquisition acceptance. Any later integration must use the existing preview, evidence review, adoption, Changes, staging and commit flow.

Before bundling, require exact rights/content review, complete EN/zh-CN authored presentation, reproducible source-to-output checks and the following negative checks: multiple definitions retain separate identities; approximate/century boundaries do not become certain points; BCE conversion has no year zero; source dates and spatial references do not become polity lifespans or invented coordinates; unknown rights stay unknown. Installed discovery, temporal reading, comparisons, evidence and return navigation remain separate acceptance. Neither source research nor a small candidate passes C02 or the complete China/Japan case.
